# Channel Plan: Nostr + X + Discord

## Nostr (community + credibility)
Cadence:
- 1-2 posts/day for 14 days
- 15-20 minutes/day replying to people with aligned audiences

Content that works:
- Proof quotes + short commentary
- Clips/GIFs of v3 polish
- Starter Packs drops
- Community spotlights
- Open questions that invite real conversation

Rituals:
- Weekly "Follow Friday" with Starter Packs
- "v3 tip of the day" for 10 days

## X (reach + conversion)
Cadence:
- 4-6 posts/week
- 1 thread/week
- 2 clips/week

Content that works:
- Opinionated value positioning (without being hostile)
- Proof-first posts (quote cards)
- Short demo clips with a single CTA
- Founder voice: why Nostria exists

Execution notes:
- Pin the main v3 post
- Use one CTA per post
- Always use UTMs on outbound links

## Discord (activation + retention)
Cadence:
- Daily prompt
- Weekly live onboarding/demo

Structure:
- #start-here
- #introductions
- #v3-feedback
- #share-your-posts
- #love (testimonials)
- #events

Programs:
- Ambassadors: 10-20 early champions; co-post weekly; get spotlight + early previews
- Challenge week: "7 days of meaningful posts" with prompts
