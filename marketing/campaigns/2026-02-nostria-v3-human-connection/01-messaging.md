# Messaging: Pillars, Proof, and CTAs

## Pillars

Pillar A: Human connection over engagement traps
- Promise: Nostria exists to strengthen relationships, not maximize addiction.
- Angle: calm, meaningful interaction; delight and utility.

Pillar B: Ownership + freedom
- Promise: users own identity/data; resist censorship; transparency at the core.
- Angle: open protocol (Nostr), portability, resilience.

Pillar C: Welcoming and easy
- Promise: quick, welcoming onboarding; simple, intuitive design.
- Angle: "invite everyone in" without barriers.

## v3 framing (translate features into benefits)
Use "felt" outcomes:

- "It feels polished and fast."
- "It makes me smile when I open it."
- "Long-form publishing is smooth and reliable."
- "It is easy to recommend to friends."

## Proof blocks
Use real quotes (with permission). See `marketing/campaigns/2026-02-nostria-v3-human-connection/06-testimonials.md`.

## Objections and responses

Objection: "Nostr is too hard / too technical"
- Response: "Nostria is quick and welcoming to join. You can be up and running in minutes."

Objection: "Decentralized means messy"
- Response: "Nostria focuses on polish and reliability so the experience feels modern and effortless."

Objection: "Is this just another client?"
- Response: "It is your social network: built around connection, ownership, and freedom."

## CTAs (pick one primary per post)
Primary:
- "Try Nostria v3" (link)

Secondary:
- "Share this with one friend you actually want to talk to"
- "Post your Nostria Card" (shareable artifact)
- "Join the Discord for onboarding/events"

## Tone rules
- Confident, human, non-preachy
- No dunking on other clients
- Avoid fear-based persuasion; focus on lived benefits and values
