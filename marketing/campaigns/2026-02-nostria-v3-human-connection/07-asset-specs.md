# Asset Specs (So Design/Video Is Fast)

## Quote cards
- Sizes: 1080x1080 (primary), 1200x628 (link preview)
- Layout: big quote (max 18-24 words) + small attribution + Nostria mark + short link
- Variants: 8-12 cards from proof pulls

## Short clips
- Length: 10-20 seconds
- Format: vertical-safe (1080x1920 preferred)
- Script pattern:
  - 1s hook text (value)
  - 6-12s UI capture
  - 2s proof overlay (mini quote)
  - 2s CTA (short link)

## Demo video (60-90s)
- Story:
  - Problem: social should be human
  - Solution: Nostria on Nostr
  - Show: 3 workflows that feel polished
  - Proof: 2 quotes on-screen
  - CTA: try v3 + join Discord

## Share kit contents
- 6 images (quote cards + product shots)
- 1 short video clip
- Prewritten posts for Nostr and X
- Discord announcement

## Press kit
- Logos: light/dark versions
- Screens: 6-10 high-resolution screens
- Boilerplate: 60/120 word company description
- Contact email or form
