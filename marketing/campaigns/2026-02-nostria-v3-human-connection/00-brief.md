# Campaign Brief

Date: 2026-02-11
Campaign: Nostria v3 (Human Connection)

## One-liner
Nostria is your social network, built for human connection on the decentralized Nostr protocol.

## Why now
Nostria is at v3.0 (product is basically finished) and has enough polish and reliability to be recommended by existing users.

## Audience (prioritize)
1) Existing Nostr users who want a better client (fastest word-of-mouth)
2) Values-aligned users: privacy/open web, creators, communities that want healthier social
3) Community organizers: hobby groups, local communities, online clubs
4) Builders/dev-adjacent audiences (if relevant)

## Primary goal
Increase activated users (not just signups).

Suggested activation definition (adjust to your product):

- New user follows 5 accounts
- New user makes 1 meaningful interaction (reply/DM)
- New user returns within 7 days

## Secondary goals
- Improve D7 retention
- Increase invites sent per activated user
- Increase partner/community co-posts

## Key constraints
- Align with manifesto: no manipulation, no dark patterns
- Growth comes from delight, utility, and community

## Strategy
1) Convert manifesto into 3 message pillars with proof
2) Package v3.0 as "felt benefits" instead of feature lists
3) Create a shareable artifact and push it consistently
4) Run a 2-week posting sprint + weekly Discord live onboarding
5) Instrument with a simple funnel and UTM discipline

## Deliverables
See `marketing/campaigns/2026-02-nostria-v3-human-connection/02-materials-checklist.md` and `marketing/campaigns/2026-02-nostria-v3-human-connection/04-calendar-14-days.md`.

## Success criteria (starter)
- Activated users: +X% vs previous 2 weeks
- D7 retention: +Y points
- Partner co-posts: 10+ in first 4 weeks
- Invite conversion: baseline established + measurable improvement
