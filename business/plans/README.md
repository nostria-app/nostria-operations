# Business Plans

This folder contains business plans, investor materials, and financial projections.

## Naming Convention

`YYYY-MM-<type>-<description>.md`

Examples:
- `2026-02-business-plan-v1.md`
- `2026-03-financial-projection.md`
- `2026-06-investor-deck-notes.md`

## Templates

Use the templates in `/docs/playbooks/` as starting points.
